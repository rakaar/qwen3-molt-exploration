What this establishes

On this prompt, removing T1181 reverses the initial-answer flip caused by T2375 removal. T1181 helps sustain December’s advantage when T2375 is absent, while substantial December probability remains with both off. These interventions do not establish a universal ordinal mechanism or a direct connection between the two MOLTs.
