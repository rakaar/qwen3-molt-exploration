How the 1,000 occurrences were drawn

We sampled 1,000 occurrences without replacement from 5,912 eligible occurrences across 1,935 conversations. Each occurrence fell within the first 1,024 formatted tokens. We excluded the original dashboard conversations and every conversation in the earlier 240-case study. Two text-only annotators divided the sample into halves and assigned one label per occurrence before gates were measured. The fixed-expression group includes titles and idioms. The reported 95% intervals resample whole conversations 10,000 times. For an embedded spelling such as “underwater,” the highlighted token contains the final “r” of “water” and may include adjacent letters. The model saw the text only through that token.
