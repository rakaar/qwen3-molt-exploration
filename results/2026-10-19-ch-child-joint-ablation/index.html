Verification and limits

Ten conditions completed. All four prior single-feature answer/probability results reproduce exactly. Zero-strength hooks and restored unhooked baselines reproduce native scores exactly. All 32 saved source observations independently reconstruct the gates, low-rank outputs and applied edits on CPU; BF16 rounding matches exactly. Downstream attention remains native.

These remove approximate MOLT components from the native MLP while retaining its remainder. This pilot tests combined necessity on two prompts. Correct answers after removal do not disprove absorption, redundancy elsewhere, or information carried at other positions. In the lowercase task, the generated child token can supply information again because this test edits only the input word.
