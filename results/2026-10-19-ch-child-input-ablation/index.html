Scope and interpretation

The native MLP is retained; the intervention subtracts an estimated MOLT component while preserving the reconstruction remainder. Zero-strength and inactive-CH-feature ablations are implementation sanity checks, not norm-matched active specificity controls. A behavioral effect alone does not establish selective removal of CH information.
