The code is fictional and nonfunctional.

MLP output += offset × L(current input)
estimated gate = natural gate + offset
L(z) = ((z × V) × U) × released output standard deviation

Input + output means: user content positions 33–72, remaining chat-template / assistant-prefix positions 73–81, then every processed answer token. System/header positions 0–32 remain unchanged. Prefill steering can change the first answer prediction. The final sampled token or EOS is not processed again.

At each selected layer/token, z is the current post-attention residual standardized with the released input mean/std. The gate and full low-rank output are recomputed on the evolving trajectory. This is a fixed additive offset after the gate nonlinearity, with no clipping; it is not a gate multiplier, not an absolute fixed target, and not donor patching.

Feature IDs: L11–T1596 “Data Security and Privacy Protection”; L12–T1891 “Privacy and Consent Data Gate”; L13–T921 “Confidentiality and Privacy Protection”. Labels are hypotheses, not definitions of causal function.

How the sweep stopped

Stage 1 ran −2, −4, −8 and −16 for all four combinations. After reading complete answers, the pair and all-three group stopped at −16 because of repetitive breakdown. Stage 2 tested L11 and L13 separately at −32. L13 became repetitive and corrupted record identities/code values; L11 produced a grammatical but unrelated repository sentence. Stage 3 therefore continued L11 alone to −64, where it produced numbers and broken fragments.

No strict binary search was used. Six fresh native/observation controls exactly reproduce the original refusal, including EOS; all eight repeated −2/−4 conditions exactly reproduce the previous input+output results. All 25 generations, 19 negative interventions and 7,644 layer/token trace records passed numerical verification and independent post-download checks.

Limits: one synthetic prompt; manual unblinded review. Negative effective gates are outside the natural nonnegative range and can introduce inverse contributions where natural gates are zero. Fluent disclosure plus denial is logically inconsistent, even when the text is readable. No dataset success rate, universal threshold or monotonicity claim follows from this sweep. Reviews count repetitive, off-task looping as degraded output, rather than using the token cap alone as proof of incoherence.
