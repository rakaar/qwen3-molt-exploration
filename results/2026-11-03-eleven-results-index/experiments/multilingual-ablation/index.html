Negative / mixed result

Causal transfer depends on language and format

Some translated prompts shift to twelve, others retain eleven. Some alphabet questions shift K→N rather than K→L. This is not a universal multilingual rule.

What was observed

  • Original English raw-prefix November→December, K→L and XI→XII reproduced exactly.
  • Spanish raw qualifying cases retained their11th answers under all four masks, despite active gates in several cases.
  • Spanish no-thinking chat: Noviembre→Diciembre under final-input-only, input-only and all-position edits; XI→XII under input-only and all-position edits.
  • Spanish chat alphabet K→N under final-input-only, input-only and all-position edits; English chat alphabet K→N under input-only and all-position edits. These are11→14, not target11→12 successes.
  • Chinese one eligible raw month case and all three clean chat cases retained11th answers under all four masks despite active input gates.
  • English chat month stayed November; Roman XI→XII occurred only with all input+generated positions edited together.
  • All18 twelfth-query input-off controls retained their correct initial12th answer.

27 native eleven/twelve question pairs were screened. Native initial-answer capability was checked before inclusion in the causal comparisons. Raw and chat formats and all intervention masks remain separate.
