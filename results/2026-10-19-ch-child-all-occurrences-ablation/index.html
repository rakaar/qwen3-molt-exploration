Child MOLT ablation at input and generated tokens

Qwen3-4B · L3–T1372 · 19 October 2026

Neither answer changed after ablating at the input and every generated child token.

Two unchanged few-shot spelling prompts, four fixed conditions, greedy decoding. Full ablation subtracts the current natural estimated Child MOLT contribution from the native layer-3 MLP at input child (position 27) and every processed generated child token. The actual edited BF16 vectors and token coverage are independently checked. CH/ch answer tokens are unedited.

Timing and interpretation

The uppercase baseline emits only CH, so both nonzero ablation scopes should be identical; the audit confirms this. The lowercase baseline first emits child, followed by : ch. Editing the generated child cannot undo that emitted word, but it can change subsequent predictions.

The intervention removes an estimated MOLT component while preserving the rest of the native MLP, including its reconstruction remainder. Unchanged answers show this component was not necessary for these two answers under this intervention. They neither establish nor rule out absorbed CH information: redundancy and the native remainder may retain it. A changed answer would also require specificity controls before attributing it to CH information. This pilot has no active norm-matched controls or probe-aligned component interventions.
