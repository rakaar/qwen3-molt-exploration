What was measured?

Raw MSEsum((MOLT prediction − native MLP output)^2) / (answer tokens × 2,560)

Lower is better. The main distributions give every prompt equal weight.

Relative squared errorsum(squared reconstruction error) / sum(native MLP output^2)

The denominator is uncentered output energy. This is not variance explained.

Target RMSsqrt(sum(native MLP output^2) / (tokens × 2,560))

Describes the scale of the output being reconstructed.

Reduction vs saved-mean predictor1 − reconstruction SSE / sum((native MLP output − saved mean)^2)

Uses the frozen checkpoint’s output mean. Negative values mean worse reconstruction than this mean-only predictor.

Matched inputs and passive observation

Both models receive identical saved token IDs. Native Qwen computation remains intact; hooks only record activations. MOLT inputs are the post-attention residuals before the layer’s post-attention normalization. Reconstruction targets are native MLP outputs before residual addition. The native models run in BF16; full frozen-MOLT reconstruction runs in FP32 with TF32 disabled, with FP64 error reductions.

The MOLT was trained on the base model. Under the medical adapter, both its input representation and the native MLP mapping may change. This comparison combines those effects; it does not isolate either mechanism.

Numerical validation and its protocol revision

The first wave stopped during layer 18 validation when a near-zero coordinate differed by approximately 5.7 × 10⁻⁵, above the original 5 × 10⁻⁵ elementwise absolute tolerance. An independent full CPU float64 reconstruction gave approximately 9.1 × 10⁻⁷ relative L2 error on that sample. Vector acceptance was changed to a per-token relative L2 tolerance of 10⁻⁵ against this independent reference, in both raw and standardized coordinates. Encoder vectors must meet the same per-token relative L2 criterion, and all CPU/GPU gate masks must agree. The initial revision retained a scalar encoder test; a later inactive encoder value differed by approximately 2.0 × 10⁻⁴ while remaining approximately 0.080 from its gate boundary, with every gate mask unchanged. Encoder scalar and reconstruction elementwise comparisons now remain nonblocking diagnostics; reconstruction arithmetic is unchanged.

Layers 10 and 16 had completed and layer 18 was partial. The first wave was remeasured from the same saved native captures; the revised criteria were then used for layers 22, 25, and 29. Model weights and the FP32 reconstruction formula did not change. This report requires every layer and model to pass the revised criteria and independently checks the saved norm errors, rather than accepting a summary pass flag alone.

Corpus and limits

We selected 1,000 first user–assistant pairs from UltraChat test_sft at a pinned revision. Selection is deterministic, removes duplicate normalized prompts, excludes the 64 historical reconstruction examples, and excludes exact normalized-text matches to the medical training source. Complete user contents contain 8–192 measured tokens; recorded answers contain at least 16 tokens and are capped at their first 256 tokens. Template tokens and EOS are excluded from the primary user/answer summaries.

UltraChat’s test split is not established as held out from Qwen or MOLT training. Exact-text exclusion does not rule out semantic overlap with medical training. The source answers are recorded synthetic ChatGPT continuations, not new outputs from either tested model. This study measures local reconstruction fidelity, not answer quality, EM prevalence, or the effect of replacing native MLPs.
