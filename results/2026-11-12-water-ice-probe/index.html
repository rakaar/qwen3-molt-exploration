Water questions · activation timing · input-only removal

Does this gate help
the model answer “water”?

The simple ice-melting questions do not activate T2375—not even while the model processes its water answers. Explicitly mentioning water in the input can activate it, but removing those input contributions keeps the tested answers.

This is a six-prompt exploratory probe, plus the original month calibration. All native probe answers pass the frozen checks. We observe every supplied input and captured response token, then compare native output with removal at all positive input positions. A separate final-input-token condition is retained in the downloads.

What this supports

“Water-related activation” is not equivalent to a general water-answer detector. The basic ice-to-water questions are answered without any positive T2375 gate in these captures. Two effective removals of explicit input-water contributions leave the water/ice answers intact, with very small probability changes. This does not show a general loss of water knowledge, identify the transform’s full computation, or prove a purely lexical mechanism.

Method, scope and precision

Pinned native Qwen3-4B BF16 and L29 MOLT files, greedy decoding, chat thinking off, eager attention, TF32 disabled, 32-token limit. The observer reads the L29 post-attention residual before native RMSNorm. Standardize with the checkpoint input mean/std, compute the natural JumpReLU gate and low-rank operator, and subtract their product in native MLP-output units. The edit is FP32 followed by one BF16 cast. All native subsequent computation remains; no full MLP replacement and no generated/candidate-token edits.

Native and zero/observer results match exactly for all eight cases. Native competence is hash-locked before nonzero edits. The independent CPU audit checks gate/operator reconstruction, selected masks, actual contribution changes, exact BF16 rounding and complete-word probabilities at 28 saved vector positions. Four positive position contributions are removed across sixteen scope conditions (including month calibration); twelve conditions target only zero gates.

Word-prefix scores sum prescribed lowercase/titlecase variants with zero/one leading space, deduplicated by token sequence. Complete multi-token words are scored. These exclude punctuation, EOS, synonyms, longer answer phrases and alternative tokenizations. Word-plus-EOS scores are separate; the actual chat responses end in a period. Percentages are rounded; raw numerical values are retained. A few FP32-based sums differ from one by a few parts in 100 million. Raw completion competence concerns the initial answer word; chat competence accepts the expected one-word answer with terminal punctuation.

This is a small exploratory probe with easy questions, no unrelated norm-matched active-transform control, and no claim about water behavior in other contexts.
