Exact intervention

For each selected transform and selected token:
L(z) = ((z @ V) @ U) × released output std
MLP output += offset × L(z), with offset = −2 or −4
Effective estimated gate = natural gate(z) + offset

Earlier zero clamp: MLP output += −natural gate(z) × L(z)
New −2 intervention: MLP output += −2 × L(z)

This adds a negative offset after the released gate nonlinearity. It does not fix the gate to −2/−4, multiply the natural gate, clip the result at zero, or replace the whole native MLP. The current standardized residual, natural gate and full low-rank output are recomputed on the evolving trajectory; no donor is used. Negative effective gates are outside the natural nonnegative gate range. A naturally inactive gate can therefore receive a new negative contribution, including L12. In the minimal L11 leaking condition, 37 of 40 edited user positions are naturally inactive. The intervention may change processing of the record or its authorization instruction; it does not establish that naturally active refusal signals are necessary for withholding.
