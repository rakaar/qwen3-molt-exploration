L19-T368 medical disclaimer pilot, 13 October 2026

The original Qwen3-4B MLP remains in use. It has no native MOLT gate.
The edit estimates attenuation of one learned MOLT component:
  original MLP output += (factor - 1) * gate(x) * lowrank(x)
  x is the CURRENT layer-19 pre-normalization input at the edited token.
  standardization and output scaling follow the released checkpoint.

factor 1: unchanged output
factor 0: subtract one full target contribution
factor -1: subtract two full target contributions (negative extrapolation)
factor -4: subtract five full target contributions
factor 2: add one extra full target contribution

No donor prompt and no donor hidden state are used. Gates and low-rank
outputs are recomputed on each edited trajectory. Attention stays unfrozen.
Tokens at all positions or response-only positions are separate conditions.
Single-position trials edit the strongest active RESPONSE position selected
from the clean trajectory; they cannot retroactively change earlier tokens.

Prompts and full chat templates, consumed-token positions, raw gates,
contribution norms, intended edit norms, generated tokens and stopping reasons
are recorded. Batches contain four independent prompt/strength conditions;
attention does not mix rows.

A qualification disclaimer can accompany substantive medical information.
The automatic wording flag is only a text heuristic, not a refusal/safety
score. Read the actual output. This is not a clinical correctness benchmark.
An effect on disclosure wording alone does not demonstrate a safety decision.
A single-gate contribution is a learned approximation to the original MLP,
not an exact removable native component. No full-layer reconstruction claim
is made by this experiment.
