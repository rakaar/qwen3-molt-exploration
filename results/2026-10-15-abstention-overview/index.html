01 / GOAL & BENCHMARK

When the answer is missing,
will the model say so?

We use unanswerable questions from SQuAD2.0: a passage is supplied, but it does not support the requested answer. The instruction explicitly asks the model to use only the passage and say it cannot determine an answer when evidence is missing.

1. Provide a passageFor example, the Amazon passage discusses animals and their traits.
2. Ask an unsupported question“Various species of lizards secrete what?” The passage does not say.
3. Look for honest withholding“The passage does not mention … Therefore, I cannot determine the answer.”
4original made-up answers
+
6new cases from 80 screened questions
→
10baseline failures used for steering
Two groups, two purposes. Natural activation evidence comes from the original ten unanswerable questions: four made-up answers and six natural abstentions. The causal cohort is different: those four made-up answers plus six new baseline failures. All six new cases were fixed before observing their steering results.

“Made up” means unsupported by the supplied passage. Some such answers are true general knowledge: respiration releasing carbon dioxide is correct, but absent from that passage. This study tests passage grounding.

03 / EXACT CAUSAL INTERVENTION

Add the transform’s output.
Keep the native MLP.

For the current input z, the MOLT approximation has a component g(z) × L(z): a scalar gate times an input-dependent, rank-128 linear output. We encourage this component by adding a × L(z) to the native layer-19 MLP output.

Estimated MOLT component: g(z) × L(z)
Applied edit:             native_MLP_output += a × L(z)
Effective estimated gate: g(z) → g(z) + a

Where and when?

  • Layer 19, transform 368; IDs are zero-based.
  • Every processed output-token position, starting with output token 0.
  • Prompt positions untouched; the first output token is predicted normally.
  • Each edit can affect subsequent predictions. Attention remains unfrozen.

What does strength mean?

+16 means add 16 × L(z), keeping the native computation. It does not mean multiplying the natural gate by 16 or replacing it with 16.

The input-dependent low-rank output is recomputed on the current steered trajectory. There is no donor prompt or full MLP replacement.

Exact normalization, operator and decoding
z = (current residual after attention 19 − input_mean_19) / input_std_19
L(z) = ((z @ V_368) @ U_368) × output_std_19
native_MLP_output += a × L(z)

z is captured before the native post-attention layer normalization. The natural gate is measured for logging from the released thresholded gate approximation; the model itself retains its native MLP. To force an absolute estimated gate h, the different correction would be (h − g(z)) × L(z). That is not the addition summarized by this 7/10 result.

BF16 native Qwen3-4B; FP32 MOLT math; greedy batch-one decoding with KV cache, thinking disabled and a 96-token cap. EOS and the final capped token are not processed again. Pinned model and MOLT identities are in the evidence record.

06 / HOW STRENGTHS WERE FOUND

A sweep, then follow-ups.
Not strict binary search.

Output quality is not monotonic: a larger coefficient can create a false explanation or break coherence after a smaller one works. We therefore inspected complete answers across strengths instead of assuming one ordered threshold.

The original four cases

The bat case received a dense integer and half-integer sweep: 128 distinct additions from 0.5 to 64. Search budgets differed across the original and new studies.

The 7/10 total is descriptive, using question-specific tuning. It is not performance of a validated held-out strength-selection policy.

08 / PROVENANCE & LIMITS

What this result establishes.

Encouraging this transform’s low-rank output can causally change several selected baseline answers into coherent, grounded abstentions. These observations do not establish a universal abstention switch, selective control on answerable questions, or improvement on a representative benchmark.

Ten cases were selected because baseline generation failed. Question-specific strengths were chosen after observing outputs; the original and new cases had different search budgets. No matched unrelated-transform control or answerable-question intervention control is included here. Labels alone do not identify what a transform writes. Some dataset questions have adversarial wording, and some unsupported answers are true general knowledge.
