What this says about water-related function

T2375 removal does not break these simple two-flask bindings. That does not prove that the transform has no water-related role in other contexts, or that other pathways are not compensating. The word-copying observations reveal a tokenization/shared-prefix issue: activation on a water piece inside a longer word cannot by itself be labelled as recognizing the completed word’s meaning.

Scope, verification and limitations

Native BF16 Qwen3-4B, pinned model/MOLT weights, greedy, chat thinking off, eager attention, TF32 disabled, max 32 generated tokens. Observe the post-attention L29 residual before native RMSNorm, apply checkpoint input standardization, calculate the natural JumpReLU gate and low-rank operator in native output units, and subtract gate × operator from the native MLP output in FP32 followed by one BF16 cast. Every supplied input position is selected. All later native computation remains; no generated/candidate positions are edited and no full MLP is replaced.

Four paired native layouts pass before any nonzero edit. All native/zero outputs and scored probabilities match exactly. Baseline eligibility is hash-locked before ablations. The independent CPU audit verifies gates/operators, all selected masks, actual positive edits, word probabilities and exact BF16 rounding at 26 saved vector positions. Eight binding contributions plus one month-calibration contribution are removed. The four lexical cases are native/observer only.

Limitations: eight easy, highly confident binding answers; only two owners and two liquids; no unrelated norm-matched active-transform control; two lexical-copying failures; no causal intervention in lexical cases. Small probability effects do not identify a mechanism. All source, source hashes, failed copying outputs and exact token scores are retained.
